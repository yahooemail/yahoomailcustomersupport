Get Help With Yahoo Mail Login Problems
=======================================

If you cannot sign in to Yahoo Mail, check your account information before requesting support.

Verify:

* Your Yahoo email address.
* Your current password.
* Your internet connection.
* Your browser or application.
* Your account verification information.

You can also try clearing browser cache and cookies, updating your browser, restarting your device, or signing in from another device.

Get Help With Yahoo Mail Security Problems
==========================================

Account security issues should be addressed as soon as possible.

Signs of a potentially compromised account may include:

* Unexpected password changes.
* Emails sent without your permission.
* Unknown account activity.
* Unrecognized recovery information.
* Suspicious login notifications.

Use Yahoo's official security and account recovery resources to protect your account.

Never provide your password to someone who claims to be a support representative.

Be Careful With Unofficial Support Services
===========================================

When searching for Yahoo Mail customer support online, you may find websites or phone numbers claiming to represent Yahoo.

Be cautious with unofficial support services.

Avoid:

* Unverified customer support numbers.
* Websites impersonating Yahoo.

* Requests for remote access to your device.
* Unexpected payment requests.
* Suspicious emails or messages.

Use Yahoo's official support resources whenever possible.
